Bon Appétit, Your Majesty felt like a warm, unhurried afternoon.
The kind where nothing urgent is happening, but you’re still fully present,
comforted by small routines and gentle conversations.

## What worked
- Food as a language of care rather than spectacle
- A soft, fairy-tale tone that never tried to be clever
- Characters who felt kind before they felt interesting

## What didn’t
- The story stayed very safe throughout
- Emotional beats were predictable, even when pleasant

## Why this stayed with me
It reminded me that not every story needs tension to be satisfying.
Sometimes it’s enough to watch people feed one another, literally and emotionally,
and let that be the point.
