Watching Reply 1988 felt like being gently folded into someone else’s memories.
It moved slowly, but never felt indulgent — more like it was giving each moment
the respect it deserved.

## What worked
- The sense of community and everyday intimacy
- Parents portrayed as full, complicated people
- Humor that felt lived-in rather than written for punchlines

## What didn’t
- The love triangle dragged longer than it needed to
- Some emotional beats were repeated to make sure they landed

## Why this stayed with me
It reminded me that ordinary days are often the ones we miss the most later.
Not because they were dramatic, but because they were shared.
